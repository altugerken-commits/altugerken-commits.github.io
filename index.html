Stripping away
the unnecessary.

I am Altuğ Erken, an industrial design student at Özyeğin University with a keen interest in engineering. I strive to operate at the intersection of aesthetic form and mechanical function, creating products that are both visually striking and meticulously engineered. While continually developing my skills in my design studies, I also serve as a member of the composites and aerodynamics sub-team at Ozu Racing, specializing in the structural design and advanced manufacturing of vehicle outer shells.

Whether I am designing modular outdoor equipment or smart living concepts, my design philosophy is rooted in stripping away the unnecessary. I focus on revealing the pure essence and raw materiality of a product.
